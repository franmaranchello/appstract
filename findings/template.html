Repeat work

Four people at Halden & Reyes asked their assistants for the same things for six months. These are the jobs that repeat, the outputs they kept getting back, and the tool each one should become.

2 Mar – 18 Sep 2026

Tokens count the transcript text in each job's sessions (characters divided by 4); chat time runs from first to last turn. Scores rank the nine jobs on each measure, 5 highest. Evidence is quoted from chat-histories/json. Screenshots are real assistant and user turns, cut to fit; open one to read the whole turn. Repeat counts come from matching output shapes across every assistant turn.
